Engineering Insight: Bursty Real-World Network Losses

Why does real-world network packet loss frequently cause rejection of the Binomial model?

The Binomial distribution relies on the fundamental assumption that packet drop occurrences are memoryless, independent Bernoulli trials.

  • Queue Saturation (Tail Drop): When an intermediate router buffer is filled, entire bursts of consecutive packets are dropped simultaneously.
  • Fading Channels (Gilbert-Elliott): Wireless and cellular links transition between Good and Bad Markovian channel states.
  • Microbursts & Congestion: Real loss events are correlated in time, producing higher variance than the theoretical binomial variance np(1-p).
